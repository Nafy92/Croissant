Welcome to Croissant Bakery

We are a family-owned bakery specializing in authentic French croissants, made fresh daily with the finest ingredients.

Our pastry chef trained in Paris and brings over 20 years of baking experience to create the perfect croissant - crispy outside, fluffy and buttery inside.

  • Chocolate Filled Croissants
  • Almond Croissants
  • Savory Cheese & Herb Croissants
